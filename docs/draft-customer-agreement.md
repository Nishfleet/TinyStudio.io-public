## What we do

TinyStudio corrects the faults named in your Appraisal Record and proposal. The proposal you accept lists every change you are buying: **only the lines approved in that proposal are changed.** Each change is shown to you — before, after, and the fault it closes — before it goes live. Nothing is published without your approval.

## What we never touch

- Your legal pages and legal copy.
- Your prices and anything on your checkout or payment code.
- Your customer data.
- Your domain settings, DNS and registrar.
- Your hosting account, plan or configuration.
- Anything not named in the approved proposal.

## What we do not promise

No promise of rankings, traffic, leads, revenue or any business result. TinyStudio promises proof, not outcomes: we fix the faults in the record and show you the before and after. What the market does with a corrected site is not ours to promise, and we will not pretend otherwise.

## How long it takes, and how it ends

The Correction is delivered within the working days stated in the proposal **[confirm from #146]**, counted from the day payment, access and your named decision-maker are all recorded. If we are waiting on you, the clock pauses.

Your access to the work — the records, the Correction Record and the Proof Receipt — ends when the order closes at the Close-out. The record links remain reachable for as long as you keep them; we keep no live access to your site after that point.

The code we write or edit for you may be processed by the AI models the desk uses to prepare and check corrections. ## Refunds

- **Anything not proven by day 21 is refunded as its share.** If a change in the proposal has not been delivered and proven — the before-and-after rerun does not show it closed — the share of the price that change represents is refunded.
- **An order we turn down is refunded in full.** If, after payment, we decline the work, you get all of it back.
- Refunds are issued through Dodo Payments. Dodo only allows refunds **within 30 days of the payment**, so the refund decision is made and the refund requested inside that window. Day 21 sits inside it on purpose.
