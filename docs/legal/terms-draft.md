## 2. What these terms cover

These terms apply to (a) tinystudio.io, (b) the free Website Appraisal, and
(c) the paid Website Correction. The price, slots and delivery schedule shown
on `/pricing` form part of these terms. If these terms and `/pricing` disagree
on a number, `/pricing` wins until we republish both.

## 3. The Website Appraisal

The appraisal is free, gated, and limited to six accepted requests a month.
When the six places are taken, the intake closes to a waitlist and a named
referral code can skip that wait.

- **What you get.** One written Website Appraisal Record for the site you
  name: the faults found, the evidence for each, and the estimated monthly
  cost of each fault, worked out from the deal value and monthly lead numbers
  you gave us. A named person reviews the output before it is sent.
- **What you give us.** One website; one email; one name; the business type;
  a deal value above zero; a rough monthly lead count. No attack of a welcome
  sequence; the only emails are the confirmation, the record link, and, if the
  paid correction was bought, the drift alerts you asked for.
- **What it is not.** The appraisal is an opinion on documents. It is not a
  contract of employment of a developer, it is not legal advice, it is not a
  re-execution of your site, and it is not a purchase.
- **Time limit.** We aim to send the record within five working days of an
  accepted request. `NISH:` Confirm 5 working days is the promise to keep
  (the code's confirmation email currently says five working days).
- **Refusals and limits.** We may refuse or stop an appraisal where the site
  is not a high-ticket service site or where the free intake was abused
  (repeated submissions from the same address or IP in one day). An abused
  request is refused; nothing was paid, so there is nothing to refund.

## 4. The Website Correction ($10,000 per site)

- **Price.** $10,000 (USD) per site, fixed, no retainer, no add-ons. Payment
  runs on Dodo Payments, whose pages take the card. We never see or store the
  card details on this site.
- **What it buys.** Fixing the faults in the Appraisal Record and presenting
  the result as before/after, in the following order: the correction, then a
  rerun appraisal, then the Proof Receipt, then fourteen days of tracking, and
  the Close-out with a referral code.
- **What it does not buy.** A new brand identity, a new site build from
  scratch, paid advertising, ongoing content marketing, a monthly retainer,
  or any promise of a revenue, ranking, booking, or sales outcome.
- **Time limit.** From the day both payment is cleared and you give access,
  the correction takes ten working days. `NISH:` Confirm the ten-working-day
  promise and spell out the remedy if we miss it (refund? partial refund?
  extension?).
- **What we need from you.** Access to the site and its host, and one
  decision-maker we can reach. If we do not get access within `NISH:` days of
  payment, work does not start and `NISH:` what happens to the payment (hold,
  refund minus bank fees, expiry).
- **Work is reviewed by a person.** Every change is written up as a line, and
  a reviewer approves or refuses the line before it is recorded. We never send
  email on the customer's behalf, never publish without your approval, never
  spend your money, and never change legal copy without your written consent;
  the correction line shows before and after for anything touching legal copy.

## 5. Ownership, access, and approval

- `NISH:` State who owns what: the site stays yours, the written record,
  correction lines, proof receipts and close-out are yours to keep and reuse;
  what TinyStudio keeps (methodology, costing tables, templates) stays ours.
- Approval gates are stated in the record: no publish without your approval,
  no spend changes, no legal-copy changes without written consent. If you
  want wider or narrower rights to that documented work, `NISH:` say so here.

## 6. Changes to publication of the work

Your Appraisal Record and its Proof Receipt live at an unguessable
`/records/<id>` link, marked noindex, reachable only by the link we send you.
They are not listed anywhere public unless you say yes at the Close-out: the
specimen gallery on `/specimen` lists a proof pair only for the clients who
consented through the desk. You can withdraw the consent later and the record
leaves the gallery. `NISH:` Confirm the withdrawal mechanism matches the
process in the privacy notice.

## 7. What we do not promise

The core sentence from the offer: TinyStudio promises proof, not outcomes. We
do not promise revenue, rankings, bookings, calls, sales, ROAS, or any
business result. The monthly-cost figure in the record is an estimate built
from published conversion benchmarks and your deal value and lead numbers; it
is a way of ranking the faults, not a forecast. Nothing on this site is a
guarantee, and nothing on `/pricing` makes one.

## 8. Acceptable use

Do not submit the appraisal form more than the daily limits allow, do not
submit a site you do not have the right to have appraised, and do not submit
someone else's email. Requests that pass limits from a single IP or address in
one day are refused. `NISH:` Add anything else you want to refuse (abuse of
the referral-code loop, scraping the specimen wall, etc.).

## 9. Payment terms

- The price is charged once per site, through Dodo Payments, in the currency
  shown on `/pricing`.
- Dodo's own payment terms apply on their checkout pages. We are notified
  through webhooks. If a webhook shows the payment failing or reversed we stop
  work on that engagement until the payment settles.
- `NISH:` Refund policy (is any part refundable? in which window? does the
  access requirement reset it?), and who eats payment-processor fees.
- `NISH:` Tax handling: who the seller of record is, whether the price
  includes or excludes applicable VAT/GST/sales tax, and what invoices exist.

- To the extent it can be limited under the governing law, TinyStudio is not
  liable for lost revenue, lost leads, lost rankings, lost deals, or lost
  profits arising from an appraisal, a correction, or a decision you make from
  them.
- The total liability of TinyStudio under these terms is capped at
  `NISH:` amount (the fee you paid? another sum?), except where the law
  cannot cap liability (Intent, fraud, death, personal injury, and whatever
  the governing law refuses to let a contract exclude).
- The record is an opinion on the state of the pages we looked at, on the day
  we looked. Sites change on their own; a fault we named may come and go.

The site and the free appraisal may pause without notice (for example when the
six slots of a month are taken). We may change these terms; the terms in force
for a paid correction are the ones published on the day you pay `NISH:`
