What we keep when you ask for an appraisal.

Draft legal text for fleet-ops#1233. Not final. Nish reviews wording before this counts as published policy.

What we collect

The appraisal form on tinystudio.io asks for a work email and a website domain. Those two fields are stored so we can send the written appraisal. We do not ask for a password, payment card, or page-login on that form.

Where it lives

The email and domain are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database used for TinyStudio email signups. Hosting is on Cloudflare Workers. We use the address to send the appraisal and to tell you if the month is already full.
