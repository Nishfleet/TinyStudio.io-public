What we keep, and when we delete it.

Draft legal text for #140 and #156. Not final, not published. Nish reviews wording before this counts as published policy.

What we collect

The appraisal form on tinystudio.io asks for a work email and a website domain. Those two fields are stored so we can send the written appraisal and tell you if the month is already full. If you buy the Correction or a monthly plan, we also keep your engagement record and the payment status Dodo Payments confirms to us. We never see or store your card details; checkout runs on Dodo Payments.

Where it lives

Requests, engagement records and access records live in a Cloudflare D1 database. Hosting is on Cloudflare Workers. We use your address to send your records and, at most, one drift note every 90 days if your site has drifted. There is no public endpoint for reading collected emails.

Access keys we hold, and when we delete them

To do the work you have ordered, you may give us access — a staging login, a deploy key or similar. We hold only what the kickoff checklist lists, use it only for the work in your proposal, and delete every key when the order closes. If you cancel a monthly plan, the keys are deleted when the paid month ends. We keep no live access to your site after that.

Requests that arrive from SEO Fix Kit

Some appraisal requests arrive from SEO Fix Kit, our sister tool. If you ask SEO Fix Kit to hand your request to the TinyStudio desk, we receive the same two fields the form here asks for — your work email and website domain — plus your audit record. It is used for the same purpose, the appraisal and the proposal, and nothing else. It is not sold and not shared with anyone else.
