The offer this covers

The Website Appraisal is free. It is a written leak audit of one page you name, and it is yours to keep. The paid desk closes what the audit finds: the one-time Correction, and the monthly plans listed on /pricing. When you complete checkout you accept the customer agreement, of which these terms are part.

What we change, and what we never touch

Only the lines approved in your proposal are changed. Each change is shown to you — before, after, and the fault it closes — before it goes live, and nothing is published without your approval. We never touch your legal copy, your prices, your checkout or payment code, your customer data, your domain settings or your hosting.

What we do not promise

No revenue, ranking, traffic, booking, or sales-volume guarantees. The desk names faults and closes them, and shows you the before and after; it does not promise more customers.

Monthly plans and cancelling

Monthly plans bill at the start of each month for the work that month. Cancel any time before your next renewal date and the next month is not charged; the month you have already paid for is delivered. There is no minimum term and no cancellation fee. A cancelled plan keeps nothing running: access to the desk's work on your site ends when the paid month ends.

Access keys

We hold only the site access the kickoff checklist lists, used only to do the work in your proposal. When the order closes, every access key we hold is deleted. If you cancel a plan, the keys are deleted when the paid month ends.

Refunds

Anything in your order that we have not proven by day 21 — the before-and-after rerun does not show it closed — is refunded as its share of the price. An order we turn down is refunded in full. Refunds are issued through Dodo Payments, which allows refunds only within 30 days of the payment; the refund decision is made and requested inside that window, and day 21 sits inside it on purpose.
