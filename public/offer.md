## Current Offer

The Website Appraisal is the free check of your site — the free leak audit of high-ticket service homepages, human-reviewed, not autonomous software. It reads the site the way a ready-to-buy customer does, names each fault in order of what it costs you, with the fix beside each, and it is yours to keep and to hand to any developer, including one who is not us. The request for a fix is made inside that report.

No call at any point. The report lands in your inbox; there is no meeting, no onboarding sequence and no call to book.

Six appraisals a month, done by hand. When the sixth is taken, the intake closes until the next.

TinyStudio then fixes what the check finds and proves the fix. Four ways to buy:

| Step | Price | What the customer gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repair | $149, $349 or $699, once | We do the fixes: up to 10 simple fixes, up to 30, or everything in the report. 90 days of watching afterwards. |
| Care | $299 a month | Fixes stay fixed. Two page improvements a month. A monthly check of what ChatGPT says about you. A monthly proof record. |
| Growth | $599 a month | Care, plus four new or improved pages a month and a weekly AI check on more questions. |
| The Website Correction | $10,000 per site, once | The full record flow — appraisal, proposal, correction, proof, tracking — for the site that carries the whole business. Six a month. |

Each fix is proven by the re-check or refunded. How the work runs: free check, fixed-price offer inside the report, fixes on a staging copy first with the customer approving each change, then the re-check and the before-and-after record. Cancel any time on the monthly plans; no contracts. Prices are starting points and will be reset by the first buyers.

What we refuse: bulk AI articles, bought links, llms.txt sold as a fix, and any promise of rankings.

A "conversion audit" search intent is answered truthfully: the free check is the leak audit — each fault named in order of what it costs you, with the fix beside it — and TinyStudio is not sold as a conversion audit service, so no conversion lift is promised. Nor is it a domain-value estimate: no domain is priced and no resale value is estimated.

No customer count, testimonial or before-and-after is published that did not happen; there are none yet. Published research stands in instead, and every number on the site carries its source.
